About Us

Smith's Dealership is a local used car business that strives for affordable vehicles, helpful advice and the highest quality of customer service. Our goal is to make the process of choosing a car as easy and convienient as possible for every customer.

We know that buying a car is a big decision and we respect that, so we try to give you clear info, support, and a friendly experience from the moment you walk through our doors.

Our Team

Our History

Smith's Dealership was founded back in 2021 by Michael Smith, he wanted to create a local dealership that could offer dependable cars and services at an affordable price. This business began with just a small selection of vehicles and a focus on helping customers feel confident in their choices.

Then in 2023, the dealership expanded to a larger location allowing us to increase our selection of vehicles available. Since then the business has continued to grow exponentially and build a positive reputation for providing a valuable, reliable and straightforward customer experience.
